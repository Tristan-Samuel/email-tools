Two different passwords

Inbox Tools account password — what you set when you sign up. You use this to log in to this app.
Mailbox App Password — what Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, or iCloud gives you after you turn on 2-Step Verification. You paste this on the Accounts page to connect your inbox. It is not the same as your normal email password.

Gmail
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account.
  2. Open App passwords (Google may ask you to sign in again).
  3. Create a new app password — name it Inbox Tools.
  4. Copy the 16-character password Google shows (spaces do not matter).
  5. In Inbox Tools, go to Accounts → Add Account, enter your Gmail address, and paste the App Password.

School or work Google accounts (Google Workspace) use the same App Password steps. IMAP host is imap.gmail.com — not imap.yourschool.org. Admins can disable App Passwords for the organization.

Outlook / Hotmail / Live
  1. Sign in at Microsoft account security.
  2. Enable two-step verification if it is not already on.
  3. Under Advanced security options, create an App password.
  4. Paste that password when adding your account in Inbox Tools (host outlook.office365.com, port 993).
Yahoo
  1. Open Yahoo Account Security.
  2. Enable two-step verification.
  3. Generate an App password and paste it in Inbox Tools.
iCloud
  1. At appleid.apple.com, sign in and open Sign-In and Security.
  2. Create an App-Specific Password for Inbox Tools.
  3. Use host imap.mail.me.com, port 993.
Proton Mail
  1. Install Proton Bridge on your computer and sign in.
  2. In Bridge, copy the IMAP credentials it provides.
  3. In Inbox Tools use host 127.0.0.1 and port 1143 (auto-filled for Proton addresses).
