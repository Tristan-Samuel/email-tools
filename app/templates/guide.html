Today

Your home surface: Do now (replies and deadlines), a curated FYI digest, and Waiting on them. Done, Snooze, and Draft reply without opening every message.

AI Search & actions

AI Search in the nav asks questions and extracts tasks from your mail (assignments, waiting on you, this week). Keyword search lives on All mail.

Quick action chips jump straight into a prompt. Assignments keeps due work on a board.

All mail & keyboard

Browse archive with tags and split-pane preview. On All mail: j/k move, e hide, u unread. Press ? for shortcuts; Ctrl+K opens the command palette.

Tags & hidden mail

Tags filter your inbox. Marketing/Newsletters can auto-hide; School never auto-hides. Review buried mail on Hidden.

Open in Gmail / Outlook

Inbox Tools triages and summarizes — you reply in your real mail app. Use Open in Gmail (or Outlook) on a message or AI draft; compose opens in your current browser, not a separate mail handler.
